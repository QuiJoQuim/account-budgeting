This module compares the budgets of ``account_budget_oca`` with the
actual analytic amounts, month by month, over the whole analytic plan
hierarchy.

Plan budgets are envelopes
--------------------------

With ``account_budget_analytic_plan``, a budget line can target an
analytic plan instead of an analytic account, for instance when the
revenue expected from a business line is known before its projects are.
Such a budget line is an **envelope**: the budget lines on the analytic
accounts and on the subplans below it, in the same budget and budgetary
position, identify part of it instead of adding up. The rest of the
envelope shows as a **Pending to identify** item of the plan.

So the budget of a plan is its envelope (or the sum of its content when
it has none) and nothing is counted twice. As projects get sold and
budgeted, their budget moves from the pending item to their account and
the total of the plan does not change. When the identified budget
exceeds the envelope, the pending item becomes negative.

Budget and actual amounts
-------------------------

- The budget lines are spread over the months of their period in
  proportion to their days, as their theoretical amount.
- The actual amounts are the analytic lines counted by the analytic
  reports (see the personnel cost source of
  ``account_analytic_dashboard``), classified by the budgetary positions
  of each budget: an analytic line belongs to the position holding its
  financial account, or to no position.
- Revenue and expenses follow the sign of the budget lines and of the
  analytic lines.

Within a budget, the budgetary positions cannot share financial
accounts, or the actual amounts would be counted twice. Different
budgets can use different positions.

Reports
-------

- **Accounting > Reporting > Budget vs Actual**: a pivot with the plan
  hierarchy down to the budget items in rows, and the budget, actual and
  deviation of each budget in columns.
- **Dashboards > Budget**: key figures against the budget, budget and
  actual by month, by line (first level of the project plan, with the
  revenue pending to identify) and by budgetary position. Filter it by
  budget: the figures of several budgets add up, and the dashboard warns
  about it.

Usage
=====

1. Define the budgetary positions, for instance one for the sales
   accounts and one for every group of expense accounts.
2. Create a budget and add its lines: on an analytic plan for the
   envelopes of the business lines, and on analytic accounts for the
   projects already known. Revenue is positive and expenses are
   negative.
3. As new projects are known, add budget lines on their analytic
   accounts: they are taken from the envelope of their plan.
4. Review **Accounting > Reporting > Budget vs Actual** or the
   **Budget** dashboard, filtered by budget.

The dashboard is generated by ``tools/build_dashboard.py``: to change
its layout, edit the script and run it again to update
``data/files/budget_dashboard.json``.
