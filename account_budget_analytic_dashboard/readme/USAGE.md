1. Define the budgetary positions, for instance one for the sales accounts and
   one for every group of expense accounts.
2. Create a budget and add its lines: on an analytic plan for the envelopes of
   the business lines, and on analytic accounts for the projects already known.
   Revenue is positive and expenses are negative.
3. As new projects are known, add budget lines on their analytic accounts: they
   are taken from the envelope of their plan.
4. Review **Accounting > Reporting > Budget vs Actual** or the **Budget**
   dashboard, filtered by budget.

The dashboard is generated by `tools/build_dashboard.py`: to change its layout,
edit the script and run it again to update `data/files/budget_dashboard.json`.
