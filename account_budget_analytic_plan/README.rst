This module extends OCA budgets with analytic-plan budget lines.

Budget lines can target either an analytic account or an analytic plan.
When a budget line targets an analytic plan, practical amounts are
computed from all analytic accounts belonging to that plan and its child
plans.

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

On a budget line, set either **Analytic Account** or **Analytic Plan**.

Use **Analytic Plan** when the budget is known at area or business-line
level before the concrete analytic accounts or projects are created.
